## Design specificity verdict

The look is authored and could not be moved to another product: cut paper, handwriting, the postcard form, three clickable example sites. The content under it is interchangeable agency copy, and nothing says who Lumina is: no person, town, price, client or phone number.

Detector: 42 findings, 31 verified, 10 false positives, 1 intentional. 24 of the verified ones share one cause: text of 6-10px inside the three example sites. Browser overlay was not available.

## Priority issues

- [P0] The conversion path is a dead end. Seven calls to action lead to a form that sends nothing and says so; there is no phone, email or other contact anywhere. Fix: connect the form, add a success state, put a phone number and email beside the form and in the footer. Command: /impeccable harden
- [P0] The portfolio contradicts itself. "Идеи, които вече са онлайн." sits over four invented brands, with notes written to the site owner visible to visitors, and the same four photos reused from the hero and examples. Fix: retitle as concepts and remove owner notes, or cut the section until real work exists. Command: /impeccable clarify
- [P1] No trust substance: no name or face, no price anchor, no "what happens after the demo", no privacy line on a form collecting personal data. Fix: one compact block before the form (who, three steps, price range) and a privacy line. Command: /impeccable shape
- [P1] Services are told twice in two taxonomies, and the first telling (the four tiles) sits between the hero and the strongest section. The 01-06 numbering implies a sequence that is not one. Fix: remove or demote the tile strip, collapse the six services, one outcome line each. Command: /impeccable distill
- [P2] Demo CTA has too many voices: five labels, several styles, the submit button is the palest button on the page, the hero note clips its text at 390px and jiggles forever. Fix: one label, one primary style, strongest button at the form, stop the jiggle after a few cycles. Command: /impeccable polish
