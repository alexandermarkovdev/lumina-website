Tilt everything a little

Paper pieces sit at −7° to +6°, most between −2° and +1°. Nothing is perfectly straight except the text blocks and the four service cards.

Layer like a collage

Shapes overlap: a browser on a blue blob, a phone on the browser, a note on the corner. Small soft shadows only on the "objects" (screens, cards).

Straight cut edges, no curves

Shapes are polygons that look cut with scissors. No rounded blobs, no circles (except stamps and step numbers), no gradients.

Do

  • Off-white paper background (#FFFEFA) with lots of empty space
  • One huge, heavy, tightly spaced headline in near-black, sentence case, ending in a full stop
  • Flat cut-paper shapes in yellow, pink, sky blue, pale blue, each slightly rotated
  • One short handwritten note (Caveat style) with a thin hand-drawn arrow
  • Real-looking website screens in a browser or phone frame, overlapping a paper shape
  • Logo „LUMINA.“ (thin wide capitals, yellow full stop) small in a corner, plus luminastudio.bg

Don't

  • Gradients, glows, neon, glossy 3D, glassmorphism, dark mode
  • Stock-photo people pointing at laptops, handshakes, robots, "AI" imagery
  • Rounded bubbly blobs, perfectly straight grids of icons, drop-shadowed text
  • More than one handwriting line, or long text in handwriting
  • Colours outside the palette (purple, orange, green as accents)
  • English slogans on Bulgarian posters, or two-word punchlines
