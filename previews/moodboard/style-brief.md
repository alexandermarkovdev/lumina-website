# Lumina: style brief for image tools

Upload `board-1.png` … `board-5.png` with this text. The short prompt at the bottom can be pasted on its own.

## Who it is for

Lumina Studio (luminastudio.bg) is a small web-design studio in Bulgaria. It builds websites for local businesses: restaurants, hair salons, barbers, shops, guesthouses. The posters speak to the owner of such a business, in Bulgarian.

## The look in one sentence

A friendly paper collage: big black heavy headlines on warm off-white paper, flat cut-paper shapes in lemon yellow, candy pink and sky blue, each turned a few degrees, with real website screens in browser and phone frames and one handwritten note with a thin hand-drawn arrow.

## Colours (flat, no gradients)

| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Paper | #FFFEFA | background, about 60–70% of the poster |
| Ink | #0B0C0E | headlines, text, arrows, thin lines |
| Lemon yellow | #FFE74C | main accent: notes, buttons, marker under key words |
| Candy pink | #FBC4D7 | second paper colour: notes, tags, strips |
| Sky blue | #45B4F7 | big background shape, postcards |
| Pale blue | #C7E8FA | quiet paper: tags, strips |
| Warm grey | #F4F2ED | browser windows, panels |
| Muted ink | #35343C | small secondary text |

## Type

- Headlines: Manrope ExtraBold (800), very tight letter-spacing (about −0.07em), line-height 0.9, sentence case, ending with a full stop. Huge.
- Section headings: Manrope Bold (700), one phrase with a flat marker bar behind the lower half of the letters (yellow, pink or blue, tilted −1°).
- Handwriting: Caveat SemiBold (600), only for one short note, rotated −2° to −7°.
- Small labels: Manrope 800, ALL CAPS, wide spacing (+0.19em).
- Body: Manrope 400–500.
- Logo: „LUMINA.“ in thin, widely spaced capitals with a yellow full stop. Small, in a corner, with luminastudio.bg.

## Shapes and marks

- Cut-paper polygons with straight scissor-cut edges (6–8 corners), flat colour, rotated between −7° and +6°.
- Paper slips: long rectangles with slightly uneven corners, stacked, alternating ±1°, in yellow, pink, pale blue, blue.
- Hand-drawn marks: thin ink lines with round ends (straight arrow, swoosh arrow, hook arrow), three short ink dashes next to a note, numbers in hand-drawn circles.
- Objects: browser window (warm grey bar, three dots in pink/yellow/blue) and black phone frame showing real websites; a cream postcard over a blue card; a yellow postage stamp „0 € демо“.
- Collage layering: screens overlap a paper shape, a note overlaps a screen corner. Soft small shadows only on screens and cards.

## Photography (only inside screens or tiles)

Warm natural light, real places, close and appetising: Neapolitan pizza, long wavy hair in a salon, terracotta roof tiles, a sea-view terrace, a desk with laptop and plant on pink.

## Voice (Bulgarian copy)

Plain, short, friendly sentences, as a friend would say it to a small-business owner. Examples: „Сайт за вашия бизнес, изцяло от нас.“ · „Хората ви търсят в Google.“ · „Безплатно демо до 24 часа.“ · „Всичко за сайта ви, на едно място.“
Main messages: people find you on Google (SEO), online bookings and orders, a website made to measure, domain, hosting and support handled, free demo within 24 hours.

## Avoid

Gradients, glows, neon, glossy 3D, glassmorphism, dark backgrounds; stock photos of people pointing at laptops, handshakes, robots or "AI" imagery; rounded bubbly blobs; colours outside the palette (purple, orange, green accents); more than one handwritten line; English slogans; two-word punchlines like „Бързо. Модерно.“
